Institutional Roots

An Educational Movement Founded by Pope Francis

Scholas USA brings young people into meaningful dialogue, fostering authentic Catholic identity through active community service. In central Los Angeles, we work alongside parish leadership to keep young Catholics engaged and supported beyond confirmation.

“I want to thank Pope Francis for starting Scholas, and especially for coming to Los Angeles. I hope this can reach all across the United States.”
· Archbishop José H. Gómez, Archdiocese of Los Angeles
